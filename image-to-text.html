About Image to Text OCR - Free AI Tool

PakTools Free Image to Text OCR uses advanced Tesseract.js AI to extract text from any image, photo, screenshot, scanned document, or book picture in seconds. Perfect for students in Pakistan to copy text from books and notes, office workers to digitize documents and invoices, and converting handwritten notes to editable text. It supports multi-language OCR: English, Urdu (Nastaliq), Hindi, Arabic + English combined. Simply upload JPG, PNG, WEBP image or screenshot, select language, and click Extract Text Now - AI will read image with progress bar and show editable text. Works directly in your browser using client-side OCR, no upload to server, so your private documents stay 100% secure. No signup, no watermark, unlimited free use. Copy text to clipboard, download as TXT file, see char & word count. For best accuracy, use clear, high-resolution images with good lighting and avoid blurry photos.
