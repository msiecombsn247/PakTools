4. Contain vs Cover vs Stretch - Konsa Best?

Contain (Recommended for PPSC): Poori image dikhegi, side me white margin rahega. CNIC ka koi hissa nahi katega. Best for documents.

Cover: Page poora fill hoga, image thodi crop ho sakti hai. Passport photos ke liye best.

Stretch: Image ko khinch ke page jitna bada kar dega. Kabhi use mat karo documents ke liye.

5. PDF Size Zyada Ho Jaye To Kya Karein?

Agar PDF 2MB se zyada hai to PPSC reject kar dega. Solution:

  • Quality ko 80% par set karo (file 50% kam ho jayegi, quality HD rahegi)
  • Pehle images ko Image Compressor se 200KB par compress karo, phir PDF banao
  • Max 5-6 images ek PDF me rakho, 20 images ek sath mat banao
