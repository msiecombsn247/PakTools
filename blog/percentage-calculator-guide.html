Pakistan Specific Formulas

1. Matric/FSC: (Obtained / Total) x 100 = %. Example 900/1100 = 81.81% A+.
2. PPSC Merit: Written 50% + Interview 50%. If 70 written, 80 interview = 75 merit.
3. Discount: Daraz 30% off on Rs 2000 = Save 600, Pay 1400.
4. GST Pakistan 17%: Cost 1000 + 17% = 1170.
5. Profit: Shopkeeper buys Rs 500, wants 20% profit = Sell 600.
