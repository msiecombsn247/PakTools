Background Remover Guide - Transparent, White, Blue BG for Passport & Amazon 2025

📅 Oct 2025⏱️ 5 min readHD PNG

🎯 Quick Answer: Passport ke liye white background, govt jobs ke liye blue background, aur Amazon/Daraz products ke liye transparent PNG chahiye hota hai. Zyada tar free tools background kharab kar dete hain (baal kat jate hain). PakTools PRO me Tolerance + Edge Feather + Manual Brush hai jo baal aur dupatte ko safe rakhta hai, 100% offline.

1. Pakistan Me Background Kaha Use Hota Hai?

  • Passport Size Photo: White background lazmi hai (ICAO rule). Off-white ya cream reject ho jata hai.
  • PPSC / FPSC / Police: White ya light blue background accept hota hai.
  • Daraz / Amazon Products: Pure white (255,255,255) background lazmi hai, warna listing reject.
  • CNIC / Document Scan: Background remove karke white karne se print clear aata hai.
  • CV / Resume: Transparent PNG se CV professional lagta hai.

2. 3 Modes Ka Farq - Konsa Kab Use Karein?

🤍 WHITE MODE (Passport ke liye)

Ye mode 200+ brightness wale pixels ko remove karta hai. Matlab white ya halka gray background 1 click me gayab. Passport selfies ke liye best.

🎨 COLOR PICKER MODE (Products ke liye)

Aap background par click karte ho (e.g., green chadar, neeli deewar) aur tool usi color ko remove karta hai. Daraz sellers ke liye best.

💚 GREEN SCREEN MODE

Agar aapne green kapre ke samne photo li hai to ye mode sirf green color ko remove karega, baaki sab safe rahega.

3. Tolerance Kya Hai? 90% Log Yahi Galti Karte Hain

Tolerance ka matlab hai kitna color remove karna hai.

Low Tolerance (0-20): Sirf pure white remove hoga. Agar background me shadow hai to nahi hatega, lekin kapre safe rahenge. Baal wali photos ke liye use karo.

High Tolerance (50-80): Halka gray, shadow sab remove ho jayega, lekin safed kapre bhi gayab ho sakte hain. Sirf product photos ke liye.

Best Setting for Passport: Tolerance 25 + Feather 2 - PakTools ka default yehi hai.

4. Manual Brush - Jab AI Galti Kare

Koi bhi AI 100% perfect nahi hai. Kabhi kabhi dupatta ya baal ka hissa cut jata hai. Is liye humne 2 brushes diye hain:

  • Erase BG Brush: Jahan extra background reh gaya ho, usko mitane ke liye.
  • Restore Brush: Agar chehra ya kapra galti se cut gaya ho to wapas lane ke liye.

Brush size 2px se 100px tak - Baalon ke liye chota brush, bade area ke liye bada brush.
