Car Loan Calculator Pakistan 2026 - Complete Auto Finance Guide

Pakistan me car lena ab cash pe mushkil hai. 2026 me 90% cars bank finance par li ja rahi hain. Alto jo 30 lac ki hai uski EMI 55,000/month se start hoti hai agar 30% down payment do to. Is guide me hum Islamic aur Conventional dono ka hisaab samjhenge.

1. Car Loan EMI Kaise Calculate Hota Hai?

Bank ye formula use karta hai jo hamare calculator me bhi hai:

EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]

P = Loan Amount (Car Price - Down Payment)
R = Monthly Markup Rate (Annual Rate / 12)
N = Total Months (Years x 12)

Example: Car 4,000,000, Down 30% = 1,200,000, Loan = 2,800,000, Rate 22% annual, 3 years (36 months)
EMI = 95,123 Rs/month, Total Interest = 624,428 Rs

3. Down Payment Kitna Lagta Hai? SBP Rules 2026

  • Suzuki Alto / Every: Minimum 20% - Bank easily de deta hai
  • Toyota Yaris / Corolla / Honda City: 30% required
  • Honda Civic / Elantra / Fortuner: 40% - 50% - High CC cars par SBP ne sakhti ki hai
  • Used Cars (2017-2022): 40% down + car ki age 5 years finance par + 9 years max age

4. Best Banks 2026 - Kaunsa Bank Sasta Hai?

1. Meezan Bank - Car Ijarah: Sabse sasta Islamic, 18% se start, lekin approval 7-10 din. 100% Halal.

2. Bank Alfalah: Sabse tez, 3 din me approval, 19.5% Islamic, used cars bhi karte hain.

3. HBL / UBL / MCB: Salary account holder ko 1% discount milta hai. Agar salary HBL me hai to HBL se lo.

Pro Tip: Bank se pehle insurance khud bahar se lo - Bank 2.5% leta hai, bahar se 1.8% me mil jata hai, 40,000 bach jayega 40 lac ki car par.

5. EMI Kam Kaise Karein? 3 Tricks

  • Tenure barhao: 3 years ki jagah 5 years lo, EMI 95K se 78K ho jayegi, lekin total interest zyada doge.
  • Down payment zyada do: 30% ki jagah 50% do, loan kam, EMI auto kam.
  • Balloon Payment: Kuch banks last me 20% lump sum lete hain, monthly kam ho jata hai.

6. Required Documents

CNIC, 6 months bank statement, Salary slip (salaried), NTN + business bank statement (businessman), 2 references, Utility bill. Filer hona lazmi hai 2026 me warna loan reject.
