Salary Guide - After Tax, EOBI 2025-26 Pakistan

📅 Oct 2025⏱️ 6 minFBR 2025-26

🎯 Quick Answer: Pakistan me 50,000 tak salary par 0% tax hai (2025-26). 100,000 par ~1,250 tax, 200,000 par ~15,500 tax. EOBI 370 Rs employee se cut hota hai. PakTools se aap Gross → Net exact nikal sakte ho, filer vs non-filer ka farq bhi.

2. EOBI Kya Hai? Kitna Cut Hota Hai?

EOBI (Employees Old-Age Benefits) - Har private employee ki salary se cut hota hai taake 60 saal ke baad pension mile.

  • Employee Share: 1% of min wage = Rs 370 (2025 min wage 37,000)
  • Employer Share: 5% = Rs 1,850 company deti hai (aapki salary se nahi)
  • Govt Jobs me EOBI nahi - Waha GP Fund cut hota hai
  • Freelancer ko EOBI nahi dena padta

3. Filer vs Non-Filer - Kitna Farq?

✅ Filer

Filer banne ke liye FBR me return file karo. Tax kam lagega. Example: 150k salary par filer tax 7,500, non-filer 11,250 (3,750 zyada).

❌ Non-Filer

Har cheez par 50% extra tax. Bank se paise nikalne par bhi tax. Salary par bhi zyada tax.

FAQs

Q: 50k par tax hai?

Nahi, 0% tax, sirf EOBI 370 cut hoga.

Q: EOBI ka faida?

60 saal ke baad pension milti hai, minimum 10,000/month 2025 me.

Q: Govt job me EOBI?

Nahi, govt me GP Fund hota hai.
