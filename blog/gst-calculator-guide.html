GST Calculator Pakistan 18% - FBR Guide 2025

Budget 2024-25 ke baad Pakistan me standard GST 18% hai. Pehle 17% tha.

1. GST Inclusive vs Exclusive?

Exclusive: Amount pe GST add karna. Shopkeepers ke liye.
Inclusive: Total amount me se GST nikalna. Customer ne 11800 diye to GST kitna?

2. Formula

Exclusive: GST = Amount x 18 / 100
Inclusive: GST = Amount x 18 / 118

3. FBR Categories

  • Standard: 18%
  • Restaurant Card Payment: 5% (PRA)
  • IT Services: 5% / 16%
  • Export: 0%
