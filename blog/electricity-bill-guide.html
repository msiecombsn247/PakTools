Electricity Bill Calculator Pakistan 2026 - LESCO K-Electric New Slab Rate Explained

Last Updated: 23 Oct 2026 | By PakTools Team | LESCO, MEPCO, FESCO, GEPCO, K-Electric, IESCO

Pakistan me har mahine 2 crore se zyada log apna bijli ka bill check karte hain. July 2026 me NEPRA ne naya slab rate jari kiya hai. Is guide me hum aapko batayenge protected vs non-protected ka farq, fuel adjustment kya hai, aur aap ghar baithe apna bill kaise calculate kar sakte hain.

1. New Slab Rate July 2026 Pakistan (Non-Protected)

2026 ka naya tariff pichle saal se 8% zyada hai. Har 100 unit ke baad rate barh jata hai:

• 1 - 100 Units: Rs. 14.16 / unit

• 101 - 200 Units: Rs. 18.12 / unit

• 201 - 300 Units: Rs. 24.21 / unit

• 301 - 400 Units: Rs. 27.15 / unit

• 401 - 500 Units: Rs. 29.96 / unit

• 500+ Units: Rs. 32.27 / unit

Example: Agar aap 300 unit istemal karte hain to 100x14.16 + 100x18.12 + 100x24.21 = Rs. 5649 sirf energy charges.

2. Protected vs Non-Protected - 50% Bill Kam Kaise?

Ye sabse important hai. Agar aapke pichle 6 mahine me har mahine 200 se kam units hain to aap Protected customer ban jate hain. Protected ka rate:

Protected 1-100: Rs. 9.5 vs Non-Protected 14.16 = 33% bachat

Protected 101-200: Rs. 12.8 vs 18.12 = 30% bachat

Tip: Agar aap 195 units pe rahenge to aap protected rahenge aur next month bhi sasta bill aayega. 201 unit karte hi aap non-protected me chale jayenge.

3. Bill ke Hidden Charges Samjhein

a) Fuel Price Adjustment (FPA)

FPA har month NEPRA lagata hai. July 2026 me average Rs. 3.5/unit hai. Ye furnace oil ki price pe depend karta hai.

b) Quarterly Tariff Adjustment (QTA)

Har 3 mahine baad lagne wala tax. 2026 me Rs. 2.1/unit. Isko khatam karne ki baatein ho rahi hain lekin abhi lag raha hai.

c) GST 18%, Electricity Duty 1.5%, TV Fee Rs. 35

Ye sab mila ke bill 22% aur barh jata hai.

4. Formula - Ghar Baithe Bill Kaise Nikalein?

Total Bill = (Units x Slab Rate) + (Units x FPA) + (Units x QTA) + Duty 1.5% + GST 18% + TV Fee

Hamare Electricity Bill Calculator Tool me aap sirf units dalen, ye formula auto calculate kar dega.

5. Bill Kam Karne ke 5 Tariqe - 40% Saving

1. 200 units se neeche rahein - Protected rahenge
2. AC ka temperature 26 pe rakhein - 15% bachat
3. Peak hours (6pm-10pm) me heavy load band rakhein - K-Electric me extra charges
4. Inverter AC / Inverter Fridge use karein
5. Solar ka net metering - 3kW system se 300 units free

6. FAQs - Log Jo Poochte Hain

LESCO bill me protected kaise check karein?

Aapke bill pe upar likha hota hai "PROTECTED" agar aap eligible hain. WAPDA ki website pe reference number daal ke bhi check kar sakte hain.

300 units ka bill kitna aayega 2026 me?

Non-Protected: Approx Rs. 8,500 - 9,200 (FPA+QTA mila ke). Protected: Approx Rs. 6,200.

K-Electric ka rate same hai?

Almost same hai, bas K-Electric me Bank Charges Rs. 8 aur thora zyada FPA hota hai Karachi ki wajah se.

Disclaimer: Ye rates NEPRA Notification July 2026 ke mutabiq hain. Actual bill me 2-3% farq ho sakta hai company ke charges ki wajah se. Source: nepra.org.pk, lesco.gov.pk - For educational purpose.
