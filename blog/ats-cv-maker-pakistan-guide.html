1. ATS CV Kya Hai? Pakistan Me Kyu Zaroori Hai?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) wo software hai jo Rozee.pk, Mustakbil, aur bade idare (PPSC, FPSC) CV filter karne ke liye use karte hain. Agar aapka CV colourful design, tables, ya images se bhara hoga to ATS usko reject kar dega.

Pakistan ke liye ATS CV me ye hona lazmi hai:

  • 1 Page Only - PPSC interviewer 6 second me CV dekhta hai
  • Simple Font - Arial, Inter, Calibri (Fancy fonts nahi)
  • Father Name, Domicile, CNIC (Optional) - Govt jobs me ye lazmi poochte hain
  • No Tables, No Text Boxes - ATS tables nahi parh sakta
  • Photo - Passport Size - 35x45mm, white background

2. PPSC / FPSC CV Ka Perfect Format

Format 1: PPSC / Government (Most Used)

Left side: Photo, Contact, Skills, Father Name. Right side: Name, Education, Experience. Ye format PPSC Clerk, Assistant, Computer Operator ke liye best hai.

Format 2: Rozee.pk / Private Jobs

Top par summary, phir skills, phir experience. Photo optional. Ye private companies ke ATS ke liye optimized hai.

4. Common Mistakes Jo CV Reject Karwate Hain

  • ❌ 2-3 pages ka CV - Pakistan me 1 page hi chahiye freshers ke liye
  • ❌ Father Name na likhna - PPSC form me father name lazmi hai
  • ❌ Gmail ke bajaye funny email (coolguy123@gmail.com)
  • ❌ Skills me jhoot likhna - Interview me typing test hota hai
  • ❌ PDF ke bajaye JPG CV bhejna

FAQs - ATS CV Pakistan

Q: Kya CV me CNIC likhna zaroori hai?

Govt jobs (PPSC) me optional hai lekin likh dena behtar hai. Private jobs me na likhein.

Q: Freshers ka CV kitna lamba ho?

1 page se zyada nahi. Experience nahi hai to Education aur Skills par focus karein.

Q: Kya ye CV PPSC me accept hoga?

Ji 100%. Ye PPSC Assistant, Clerk, Data Entry Operator ke format par bana hai.
