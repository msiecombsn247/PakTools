What is ATS CV?

ATS is a software used by Rozee.pk, Mustakbil, LinkedIn and big companies in Pakistan like Jazz, Telenor, Systems Limited. It scans your CV for keywords. If you use a fancy design CV from Canva, your score becomes 0.

  • Simple Format: No tables, no columns, no images
  • Correct Headings: Experience, Education, Skills
  • Keywords: Add skills mentioned in job ad
  • File Type: Always PDF, not JPG or DOCX image

Perfect Format for Pakistan Jobs

For Pakistan market, use this order. This format is accepted for Private, Government and Gulf jobs:

1. Header: Full Name, Phone with +92, Email, City (Lahore, Karachi), LinkedIn link

2. Professional Summary (3 lines): Example - "Motivated BSCS graduate with 1 year experience in..."

3. Education: Degree, University, Year, CGPA. Latest first.

4. Experience: Company Name, Position, Duration, 3 bullet points of work. Use numbers - e.g., "Increased sales by 20%".

5. Skills: MS Office, Communication, etc. Match with job description.

Common Mistakes Pakistani Students Make

  • Using photo in CV - Not needed for Pakistan private jobs, only for Gulf jobs.
  • Writing "CV" or "Resume" on top - Don't write it. Just start with your Name.
  • Long CV - For freshers, 1 page is enough. For experienced, max 2 pages.
  • Spelling mistakes - ATS rejects CV with spelling errors.
  • Using stylish fonts - Only use Arial, Calibri, or Inter.
