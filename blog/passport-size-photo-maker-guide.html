What is the Correct Size for Passport Photo in Pakistan?

For Pakistan, the standard size is different for different documents. If you use the wrong size, your application can be rejected.

  • NADRA CNIC / B-Form: 35mm x 45mm with white background
  • Pakistani Passport: 35mm x 45mm, 300 DPI, white background
  • Visa Application (USA, UK, Schengen): 2x2 inch (51x51mm)
  • Face Size: 70% to 80% of the photo should be your face

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many people make these small mistakes which cause rejection:

  • Do not wear white shirt, wear a dark color shirt.
  • Both ears should be visible. No side pose.
  • No glasses with reflection. Simple background is compulsory.
  • Do not crop too much. Leave some space above head.
