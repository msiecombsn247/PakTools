What is Passport Size Photo in Pakistan?

In Pakistan, the standard passport size photo is 35mm width x 45mm height (3.5cm x 4.5cm). This size is required by NADRA for CNIC, B-Form, Pakistan Passport, and most government jobs like PPSC, FPSC, and Army. For USA Visa, the size is 51x51mm (2x2 inch). The resolution must be minimum 300 DPI for printing, which means 413 x 531 pixels for 35x45mm. Many studios in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad still give low quality 150 DPI photos which get rejected online.

Why 300 DPI is Important?

DPI means Dots Per Inch. For NADRA and Passport offices, low DPI photos look blurry when printed. 300 DPI is the international standard for passport photos. Our tool exports your photo at exactly 413 x 531 pixels which equals 35x45mm at 300 DPI. This is print-ready and also perfect for online forms where file size must be under 1MB but quality must be high.

Common Mistakes That Cause Rejection

1. Blue or grey background - NADRA only accepts pure white (#ffffff).
2. Wearing glasses or cap - Not allowed for CNIC and Passport.
3. Face too small - Face must cover 70% of photo.
4. Low quality / blurry - Always export 300 DPI.
5. Selfie angle - Always take from front, not from top or bottom.

Passport Photo Requirements for Different Uses in Pakistan

NADRA CNIC / B-Form: 35x45mm, white, 300 DPI, file size < 500KB JPG.
Pakistan Passport: 35x45mm, white, no smile, ears visible.
USA / UK Visa: 51x51mm (600x600 px), white, 300 DPI.
PPSC / FPSC Jobs: 35x45mm, white, recent (last 6 months).
University Admission (Punjab University, UET): 35x45mm, white or light blue.
