2026-27 me token tax ka system badal gaya hai. Punjab aur Islamabad ab engine CC par nahi, gaari ki invoice price par tax lete hain. Sindh aur KPK abhi bhi purane fixed slabs par hain. Is guide me har province ka hisaab original excise rates se.

3. Islamabad ICT Token Tax - Sabse Mehenga

Finance Act 2026 se ICT bhi price based ho gaya 1 July 2026 se:

  • Upto 1000cc: Rs. 20,000 lifetime
  • 1001-2000cc: 0.25% of invoice per year - 40 lac ki City par 10,000/year (Pehle 1,500 tha!)
  • Above 2000cc: 0.35% - PakWheels ne estimate kiya Corolla Grande ab 19,530/year pay karegi ICT me

Payment: PAK App (ICT official) se challan banta hai.

4. Transfer Fee + Income Tax (WHT) Kitna Lagta Hai?

Token tax ke alawa 3 cheezein aur lagti hain jab car kharido/becho:

  • Transfer Fee (Excise): Punjab me upto 1000cc = 4,540, upto 1800cc = 9,075, above 1800cc = 18,150. Filer/non-filer same.
  • Income Tax (Advance Tax) U/S 231B: Filer ke liye 1300cc tak 2,500, 1600cc tak 50,000, 2000cc tak 200,000, above 2500cc 400,000. Non-filer iska 3 guna dega.
  • Professional Tax: Rs. 200 fixed

5. Electric Cars Par Kya Hai?

Punjab ne EV par 95% discount diya hai token tax me, Finance Bill me 99% karne ki tajweez hai. KPK me 30 June 2028 tak EV token tax ZERO hai. Sindh me abhi normal rates hain EV par.

6. Token Tax Online Kaise Pay Karein?

Punjab: ePay Punjab App → Search Vehicle → Pay with Easypaisa/JazzCash/Card
Sindh: excise.gos.pk → Vehicle Tax → CNIC
ICT: PAK App → Motor Vehicle Tax
