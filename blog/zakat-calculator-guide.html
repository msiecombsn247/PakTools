Zakat Calculator Pakistan 2025 - Complete Guide

Pakistan me Zakat Nisab 2025 = Rs. 105,000 (Silver basis). Agar apke paas 1 saal tak is se zyada maal raha to 2.5% Zakat dena farz hai.

1. Zakat Kis Par Farz Hai?

  • Gold 7.5 tola (87.48g) se zyada
  • Silver 52.5 tola (612g) se zyada
  • Cash, Bank Balance, Business Stock
  • Property jo bechne ke liye kharidi ho

2. Calculation Formula

Zakat = (Gold + Silver + Cash + Business + Loan - Debts) x 2.5%
