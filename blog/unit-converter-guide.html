Property Units Pakistan

1 Marla = 272.25 sq ft (Lahore standard). Karachi me 225 sq ft bhi hota hai lekin official Punjab Land Record me 272.25 use hota hai.
1 Kanal = 20 Marla = 5445 sq ft
1 Acre = 8 Kanal = 43560 sq ft
1 Murabba = 25 Acre

Weight - Maund Seer
