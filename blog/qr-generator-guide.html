QR Code Guide - WhatsApp, WiFi, Business QR with Logo 2025

📅 Oct 2025⏱️ 5 minLogo QR

🎯 Quick Answer: Pakistan me dukaan, restaurant, salon ke liye QR code sab se zyada faida deta hai. WhatsApp QR se customer direct message karta hai, WiFi QR se mehman bina password likhe connect ho jata hai. PakTools me aap logo wala HD QR bana sakte ho jo print me bhi scan ho, 100% offline.

1. Pakistan Me QR Code Kaha Use Hota Hai? Business Ideas

  • Dukaan / Shop: Counter par WhatsApp QR lagao - "Scan karke order karo". Customer ko number save nahi karna padega.
  • Restaurant / Cafe: Table par Menu QR - Scan karke menu dekhe. WiFi QR - Bina password bataye connect.
  • Salon / Clinic: Visiting card par QR - Scan karke location, Instagram, WhatsApp sab khul jaye.
  • Daraz / Online Seller: Parcel par QR - Scan karke return policy ya video guide.
  • Wedding Card: Card par Location QR - Mehman scan karke direct Google Maps par.

2. 6 QR Types Ka Farq

🔗 URL QR (Sab se zyada use)

Aapki website, Instagram, YouTube ka link. Customer scan karega to direct khulega. Example: `https://instagram.com/yourshop`

💬 WhatsApp QR (Sales ke liye #1)

Format: Number + Message. Example: Number `923001234567`, Message `Salam, mujhe ye product chahiye`. Customer scan karte hi aapke WhatsApp par ye message ready hoga.

📶 WiFi QR (Mehmanon ke liye)

WiFi Name + Password + Security (WPA). Mehman scan karte hi bina password likhe connect ho jayega. Hotels me lazmi hai.

📝 Text / CNIC QR

CNIC, bank account, address. Scam se bachne ke liye CNIC par QR lagao taake original verify ho sake.

3. Logo Wala QR Kaise Banaye? HD Print Trick

Normal QR me logo lagane se scan fail ho jata hai. PakTools me High Error Correction (H) use hota hai - Matlab 30% tak QR kharab bhi ho to scan ho jayega. Is liye beech me logo safe hai.

Best Settings for Print:

  • Size: 800px (visiting card ke liye 300px kaafi hai, banner ke liye 800px)
  • Color: Black QR on White BG - Sab se zyada scan hota hai
  • Logo Size: Auto 22% - Zyada bada logo scan fail kar dega
  • Download: PNG for social media, JPG for print shop

5. WiFi QR Se Paise Kaise Bachaye?

Har dafa mehman ko WiFi password batana padta hai - Galat likhte hain, time zaya hota hai. WiFi QR ek dafa banao, frame karke deewar par lagao. Mehman khud scan karke connect ho jayega. Password change ho to naya QR banao.
