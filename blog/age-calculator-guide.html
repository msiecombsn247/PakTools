Age Calculator Guide - PPSC Age Limit 21-28 Check 2025

📅 Oct 2025⏱️ 6 minPPSC CHECK

🎯 Quick Answer: PPSC har job me cut-off date deta hai (e.g., 1st Jan 2025 ko age 21-28 honi chahiye). Agar aap 28 years 1 din bhi zyada ho to form reject. PakTools me aap DOB + Cut-off date dal kar exact years, months, days aur eligibility check kar sakte ho.

2. Exact Age Kaise Nikalte Hain? Formula

Normal calculator sirf year batata hai, months/days nahi. PPSC ko months/days bhi chahiye. Formula:

  • Years = Till Year - DOB Year (agar birthday nahi aya to -1)
  • Months = Till Month - DOB Month (negative ho to 12 add karo, year -1)
  • Days = Till Day - DOB Day (negative ho to previous month ke days add karo)

Example: DOB 15 Aug 1997, Cut-off 1 Jan 2025 = 27 years, 4 months, 17 days = Eligible for 21-28 ✅. Lekin 1 Feb 2025 ko 27y 5m 17d = Still eligible. 15 Aug 2025 ko 28y 0m 0d = Last day eligible. 16 Aug 2025 ko 28y 0m 1d = Not eligible ❌

3. Age Relaxation Kya Hai? 90% Log Miss Karte Hain

PPSC me general age 21-28 hai lekin relaxation ke baad:

  • Male: 28 + 5 = 33 years tak eligible (Punjab Govt rule)
  • Female: 28 + 8 = 36 years tak eligible
  • Govt Servant: 10 years relaxation (with NOC)

Is liye agar aap 29 years ke ho to bhi male ke tor par 33 tak eligible ho. Ad me relaxation mention hota hai.

4. Step-by-Step - PPSC Eligibility Check

STEP 1:

STEP 2:

STEP 3:

Min 21, Max 28 set karo (Police ke liye 18-25). Agar male ho to Max 33 karke bhi check karo relaxation ke sath.

STEP 4:

Calculate > Green ✅ Eligible aye to apply karo, Red ❌ Not Eligible aye to relaxation check karo ya next ad ka wait karo.

5. CNIC Se DOB Nahi Pata? Trick

Agar CNIC par DOB saaf nahi to CNIC to DOB Tool use karo. Waha CNIC number dal kar DOB nikal jayegi.

FAQs

Q: 28 years 1 month eligible?

Nahi, general quota me nahi. Lekin male relaxation se 33 tak eligible ho.

Q: Cut-off date kya hoti hai?

Ad ki last date. Us din aapki age 21-28 ke andar honi chahiye.

Q: Police ke liye age?

Constable 18-25, SI 20-25, no relaxation.
