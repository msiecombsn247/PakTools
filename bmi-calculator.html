About BMI

BMI (Body Mass Index) measures body fat based on height and weight. Our free calculator helps you check if you are underweight, normal, overweight or obese. Ideal for fitness, diet planning, and health awareness in Pakistan. 100% free, no signup.

How to Use?

  • Select CM/KG or Feet/LBS
  • Enter height & weight
  • Click Calculate to see BMI & ideal weight
