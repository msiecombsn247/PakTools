About BMI Calculator - Health Tool for Pakistan

BMI (Body Mass Index) measures body fat based on height and weight. It is the international standard used by WHO to check if you are underweight, normal weight, overweight or obese. Our free BMI calculator is specially useful for Pakistanis for fitness, diet planning, gym, and health awareness. It supports both CM/KG and Feet/LBS units. Just enter height and weight to instantly see your BMI, health status, BMI bar and ideal weight range for your height. The tool also explains BMI chart and what your ideal weight should be. 100% free, private, no signup, works on mobile. Note: BMI does not consider muscle mass, so athletes should consult doctor.

How to Use BMI Calculator?

  • Select unit: CM/KG or Feet/LBS
  • Enter height using slider or number input
  • Enter weight using slider or number input
  • Click Calculate to see BMI, status & ideal weight
