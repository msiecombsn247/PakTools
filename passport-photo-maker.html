What is Pakistan Passport Photo Size?

Official size for Pakistan passport is 35mm x 45mm with white background, 70-80% face visible, 300 DPI. For CNIC / Nadra use 33mm x 48mm. This tool automatically crops and resizes your photo to correct size. Print on photo paper for best result.
