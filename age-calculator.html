What is Age Calculator?

Our free Age Calculator is a powerful and accurate online tool that helps you calculate your exact chronological age in years, months, and days. Whether you need to know your age for official documents, school admission, job applications, retirement planning, or just out of curiosity, this tool provides instant and precise results. Unlike simple year subtraction, our calculator considers leap years, different month lengths, and exact dates to give you the most accurate age possible. It is widely used in Pakistan, India, and worldwide for calculating age for CNIC, passport, and other legal requirements. The tool is 100% free, requires no signup, and works on all devices including mobile, tablet, and desktop. All calculations are done locally in your browser, so your date of birth is never stored or shared, ensuring complete privacy and security.

How to Use?

  • Select your Date of Birth (DOB).
  • Second date is today by default.
  • Click Calculate Age.
  • See years, months, days + total days lived.

Key Features

  • Exact Calculation: Leap years included.
  • Total Time: Shows days, weeks, hours.
  • Privacy: No data saved.
  • Free Forever: Unlimited use.
