About Age Calculator - Exact & Private

PakTools Free Age Calculator is the most accurate tool to calculate your exact chronological age in years, months, and days. Unlike simple calculators, our tool considers leap years, varying month lengths (28-31 days), and time zones for 100% precision. It is perfect for official use cases in Pakistan including school admission, university forms, CSS age eligibility, government jobs, NADRA CNIC, passport application, and retirement planning. The tool also shows total days lived, total weeks, total hours and even minutes, making it useful for fun facts and birthday planning. All calculations are done locally in your browser using JavaScript - your date of birth is never uploaded, stored, or tracked, ensuring complete privacy. It works on all devices, no app install or signup needed, and is free forever.

How to Use Age Calculator?
