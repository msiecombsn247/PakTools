About Age Calculator

Our free Age Calculator calculates your exact age in years, months, days. Perfect for official documents, school admission, job applications, CNIC, passport, and retirement planning. It considers leap years and different month lengths for 100% accuracy. 100% free, no signup, works on mobile & desktop. All calculations happen in your browser, so your date of birth is never stored, ensuring complete privacy and security. Widely used in Pakistan and worldwide.

How to Use?

  • Select your Date of Birth
  • Second date is today by default
  • Click Calculate & see results

Features

  • Total days, weeks, hours lived
  • Privacy safe, Free forever
