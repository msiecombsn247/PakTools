How CNIC Number Works?

First 5 digits: Province, Division, District, Tehsil
Example: 35202 = Lahore, Punjab

Middle 7 digits: Family Number

Last digit: Gender
Odd (1,3,5,7,9) = Male 👨
Even (2,4,6,8,0) = Female 👩

Province Codes:

1 = KPK, 2 = FATA, 3 = Punjab
4 = Sindh, 5 = Balochistan
6 = Islamabad, 7 = Gilgit-Baltistan
