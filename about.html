We noticed that most premium tools are paid or full of ads, so we decided to create a platform that is fast, secure, and works on every mobile phone.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make Pakistan digitally strong. We want every student in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Multan to have access to tools like Image Resizer for Passport Size Photos, Percentage Calculator for Matric/FSC results, BMI Calculator for health, and CNIC Information Validator. All our tools work 100% in your browser, we never upload your photos or CNIC to any server. Your privacy is our first priority.
